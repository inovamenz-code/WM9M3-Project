## Teaching framework

This is a University coursework submission derived from the RTBase teaching
renderer. The repository preserves the original teaching/source comments.
Changes made for the coursework should not be separated from the framework or
redistributed as though the whole repository were independently authored or
independently licensed.

## Third-party components

  import library, DLLs and original notices remain under
  `ThirdParty/oidn-2.5.1.x64.windows/`. The corresponding runtime notices are

  is retained because the demo uses it. Its source and redistribution terms are

course briefs, reports, solution archives and unrelated development material.
No new broad licence is granted for the combined coursework framework, bundled
assets or third-party components.
